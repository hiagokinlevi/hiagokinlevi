## Start learning

### OpenCyber Academy

[OpenCyber Academy](https://opencyber.academy/) is a free, public cybersecurity learning library. You can study without creating an account. Choose a subject or a formation level, then follow a course's guide, lessons, examples, exercises and worked answers.

**[Open the academy](https://opencyber.academy/)** · [Browse by subject](https://opencyber.academy/subjects/) · [Browse by level](https://opencyber.academy/levels/) · [Read the source on GitHub](https://github.com/hiagokinlevi/OpenCyberAcademy)

The [curriculum](https://github.com/hiagokinlevi/OpenCyberAcademy/blob/main/COURSE_GUIDE.md) spans 23 academic areas and nine formation levels: 207 course tracks, with nine language editions planned for each. English is the reference language; Portuguese and Spanish follow. **These are plans, not a claim that every course or translation is complete.** The [book standard](https://github.com/hiagokinlevi/OpenCyberAcademy/blob/main/BOOK_STANDARD.md) distinguishes published teaching from outlines and records what must be reviewed before a book is called complete.

To see an authored course, start with [rapid Incident Response in English](https://opencyber.academy/courses/rapid-1-month/incident-response-resilience/?lang=en). To study entirely from the repository, follow the [study path](https://github.com/hiagokinlevi/OpenCyberAcademy/blob/main/STUDY_PATH.md).

## Defensive engineering

Selected public work includes [incident-response playbooks](https://github.com/hiagokinlevi/ir-playbooks-automation), [AI security guardrails](https://github.com/hiagokinlevi/ai-security-guardrails) and [cloud posture assessment](https://github.com/hiagokinlevi/cloud-posture-watch).
